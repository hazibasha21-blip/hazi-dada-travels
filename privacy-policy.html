Privacy Policy

Hazi Dada Travels

1. Introduction

Hazi Dada Travels respects your privacy and is committed to handling customer information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the Hazi Dada Travels website, why it is collected, and how it may be used.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit an enquiry through our website, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • The trip you are interested in
  • Your enquiry or message
  • The date and time of your enquiry

We only request information that is reasonably necessary to respond to your travel enquiry or booking request.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the website may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Discuss trip availability
  • Confirm or process booking requests
  • Contact you regarding your requested trip
  • Provide customer support
  • Maintain booking and enquiry records

We do not use customer information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis or your permission where required.

4. Phone and WhatsApp Communication

Hazi Dada Travels may contact customers using the phone number provided in an enquiry for legitimate travel-related communication.

If you choose to contact Hazi Dada Travels through WhatsApp or another external communication service, that communication may also be subject to the privacy policy and terms of that service.

5. How We Store Information

Website enquiry information may be stored electronically using our website's backend services.

Hazi Dada Travels uses Supabase as part of its website infrastructure for storing and managing website data.

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

6. Sharing of Information

Hazi Dada Travels does not sell customer personal information.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to provide the requested travel service, comply with a legal obligation, protect the rights or safety of customers or the business, or where sharing is otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

7. Data Retention

We may retain enquiry and booking information for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, booking management, business records, legal requirements, dispute resolution, or other legitimate business purposes.

When information is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable requirements.

8. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide personal information. However, certain information such as your name and phone number may be necessary for us to respond to a booking or enquiry.

If you have previously submitted information to Hazi Dada Travels and want to ask about its use, correction, or deletion, you can contact us using the contact details provided on our website.

9. Cookies and Website Technology

The website may use essential browser storage or similar technical mechanisms where necessary for website functionality.

We do not intentionally use the website to collect sensitive personal information through unnecessary tracking technologies.

10. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services to provide hosting, database, authentication, communication, maps, media, or other website functionality.

Those services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for general travel information and booking enquiries. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Hazi Dada Travels may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page.

13. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about the personal information you have submitted to Hazi Dada Travels, please contact Hazi Dada Travels using the phone, WhatsApp, or other contact information displayed on our website.
